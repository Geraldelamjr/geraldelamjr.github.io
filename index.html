Delta Air Lines / Project case study
Ground Support Equipment Engineering

CRJ-700 Fuselage Stand

January – May 2024ATL & MCO & JFK airports

Designed a stand to support a CRJ-700 aircraft fuselage section at its normal aircraft height and orientation, allowing jet bridge operators to practice approaching the aircraft door without using an active aircraft and reducing the risk of aircraft damage.

From concept to implementation

Methodology

FIELD MEASUREMENTS

Measure

Visited JFK to obtain fuselage dimensions and establish the required support geometry.

DESIGN

Model & document

Used SOLIDWORKS to develop the 3D model, part drawings and assembly drawings.

FABRICATION

Build

Specified 2 × 2 steel tubing for the welded stand structure.

Design considerations

Extended the base forward to reduce the risk of tipping and incorporated braking casters to allow mobility while preventing unintended rollaway when secured.

New and Retrofit Baggage Valet Cart

Objective

Existing baggage valet carts did not meet the durability requirements for long-term use. The objective was to develop both a retrofit solution and an entirely new cart design with a stronger frame and reinforced structure to improve service life and reliability.

Design concepts

Methodology

FIELD MEASUREMENTS

Measure

Obtained full measurements of the existing cart, inner skeleton and shelving to be replaced.

DESIGN

Model & document

Used the measurements to develop CAD models and assembly drawings for the proposed designs.

PROJECT APPROVAL

Evaluate & present

Obtained manufacturer quotes and prepared a one-pager to present to the Project Approval Board (PAB).
