Delta Air Lines / Project case study
Ground Support Equipment Engineering · 01

CRJ Fuselage Stand

January – May 2024ATL & JFK airports

Designed a stand to support a CRJ aircraft fuselage section at its normal aircraft height and orientation, allowing jet bridge operators to practice approaching the aircraft door without using an active aircraft and reducing the risk of aircraft damage.

From concept to implementation

Methodology

01 / FIELD MEASUREMENTS

Measure

Visited JFK to obtain fuselage dimensions and establish the required support geometry.

02 / DESIGN

Model & document

Used SOLIDWORKS to develop the 3D model, part drawings and assembly drawings.

03 / FABRICATION

Build

Specified 2 × 2 steel tubing for the welded stand structure.

Design considerations

Extended the base forward to reduce the risk of tipping and incorporated braking casters to allow mobility while preventing unintended rollaway when secured.
